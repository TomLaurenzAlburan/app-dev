# Gotham: A Dark Origin Story

*Gotham* is a crime drama television series set in the Batman universe. It follows the origins of several iconic characters, including James Gordon, Bruce Wayne, and many famous villains.

## Key Characters

| Character           | Actor               | Role                                                    |
|---------------------|---------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| **James Gordon**    | Ben McKenzie        | Detective, later Commissioner of Gotham                 |
| **Bruce Wayne**     | David Mazouz        | A young boy destined to become Batman                   |
| **Oswald Cobblepot**| Robin Lord Taylor   | Crime boss who becomes the Penguin                      |
| **Selina Kyle**     | Camren Bicondova    | A skilled thief, known as Catwoman                      |
| **Edward Nygma**    | Cory Michael Smith  | A brilliant but unstable man, eventually the Riddler    |

## Ordered List

1. The story begins with the murder of **Thomas and Martha Wayne**, which sets young **Bruce Wayne** on a lifelong quest for justice.  
2. **Detective James Gordon** promises Bruce that he will find the killer and clean up Gotham.  
3. Meanwhile, **Oswald Cobblepot** rises through the criminal ranks, manipulating events to his advantage.  
4. **Selina Kyle**, a young street thief, becomes both an ally and rival to Bruce.  
5. As the seasons progress, Gotham’s most notorious villains—**The Riddler**, **Penguin**, **Joker**, and others—emerge from the city’s darkness.  
6. **Bruce Wayne** slowly transforms from a grieving boy into a determined vigilante, preparing to become **Batman**.  
7. In the final season, Gotham descends into chaos, forcing its heroes and villains to choose their sides as the city’s fate hangs in the balance.

## Strikethrough
~~It was supposed to be a typical Batman prequel~~, but *Gotham* became a story about how the villains and Gotham itself shaped Batman’s future.

## Themes of *Gotham*

- **Corruption in Gotham**: The city is a hotbed of corruption, not only in its government but also in the police force and the criminal underworld.
- **The Creation of Villains**: *Gotham* explores how iconic villains like **Penguin**, **Riddler**, and **Joker** are formed.
- **Moral Ambiguity**: Characters often struggle with the question of what is right and wrong.

## Why I Love *Gotham*

I love *Gotham* because of its unique take on the DC universe and how it explores the origins of both heroes and villains. The show delves deep into Gotham City's troubled history, making it feel alive and unpredictable.

> *"The city of Gotham is like a broken mirror, shattered yet reflecting every piece of the truth."* - **James Gordon**
